1. Information Collection

Ibrahim Enterprise collects business contact details (name, corporate email, phone/WhatsApp number, company name, destination port, and commodity requirements) submitted voluntarily through our trade inquiry forms or direct email correspondence.

Data collected is utilized strictly for evaluating trade feasibility, preparing commercial quotations (FOB/CIF), conducting phytosanitary and export compliance, and coordinating container shipments. We do not sell, license, or monetize client data.

Trade inquiries and commercial correspondence are stored in secure corporate databases and accessed only by authorized trading personnel. We employ encryption in transit and comply with standard international business security protocols.
