Asafoetida is an essential natural resin prized for its distinctive allium aroma and flavor in global spice formulations. Ibrahim Enterprise maintains direct commercial sourcing relationships across Afghanistan's mountain collection zones, handling:

  • Direct procurement of unadulterated raw gum oleoresin tears and crystalline lumps.
  • Customs bonded transport and rigorous purity evaluations at port of entry.
