Commercial Commodity Leadership

Ibrahim Enterprise operates at the intersection of traditional agricultural sourcing and modern container logistics. Our trading operations encompass two dedicated divisions:

Direct Import Corridor (Afghanistan Hing)

Importing pure raw Asafoetida (Hing) resin tears and lumps with reported handling capacity of 20 MT per month, supplying key spice processing units.

Worldwide Export Logistics (~100 Containers/Month)

Handling container consignments across Basmati rice, black pepper, green cardamom, coconut powder, dehydrated onion, fresh produce, and seafood.
