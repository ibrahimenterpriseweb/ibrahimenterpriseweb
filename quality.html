Standards & Traceability

Quality Assurance & Export Compliance

International agricultural trade demands stringent discipline. Ibrahim Groups adheres to verifiable batch sampling, physical inspection, moisture monitoring, and official phytosanitary export documentation.

01

Origin Traceability

Every shipment is linked to specific cultivator zones in Afghanistan (Hing) or verified farming clusters in India (Basmati, spices, vegetables).

02

Physical Lot Inspection

Pre-shipment batch testing for moisture limits, foreign matter exclusion, aroma profiling, seed count, and grain length elongation.

03

Cold Chain Integrity

Perishable vegetable consignments and blast-frozen seafood (-40°C) are sustained through continuous reefer container temperature logs.

04

Official Phyto & Customs

Complete dispatch dossiers including plant quarantine clearances, non-GMO declarations where applicable, and clean bills of lading.
