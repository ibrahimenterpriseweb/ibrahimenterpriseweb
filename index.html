Proposed Operational Methodology

Proposed 5-Step Sourcing & Supply Chain

Our structured trade framework guiding agricultural commodities from farm and origin partners to international ports of arrival.

01

Supplier Sourcing

Direct origin engagement with verified spice cultivators in Afghanistan and agricultural grower hubs across South India.

02

Quality Inspection

Moisture grading, aroma evaluation, purity testing, grain length verification, and physical inspection prior to batch approval.

03

Packaging

Multi-wall food-grade barrier bags, aroma-sealed vacuum packs, BOPP sacks, or customized buyer private-label packaging.

04

Documentation

Commercial invoicing, phytosanitary certifications, bill of lading, certificates of origin, and import clearance documentation.

05

Shipment

Dry and temperature-monitored reefer container bookings with premier shipping lines targeting international destination ports.
